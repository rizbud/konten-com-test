# Approval is final

Approving a submission pays the creator there and then — an earning is settled
money, not an obligation waiting on a later payout stage, which is also the only
reading the schema can store. Views can fall afterwards when a platform scrubs
fake views, and we do not adjust, reverse, or claw back an earning when that
happens.

## Consequences

The creator cannot control platform view-scrubbing, so retroactive debt would
make their income unpredictable — the fastest way to lose the supply side. The
brand carries the decay on work already paid for. The lever for reducing that
exposure is on the approval side, not the reversal side: approve once views have
settled, or price a decay reserve into the campaign. Views at Approval exists to
make the basis of every past payment auditable when the live count no longer
matches it.
